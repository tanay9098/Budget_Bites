# Soy chunk pulao

A vegetarian rice dish with soy chunks (soya nuggets) as the protein. Contains soy.

**Serves 2. Prep 15 min, cook 20 min. Equipment: pressure cooker on a gas stove or induction cooktop.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Rice 120 g
- Soy chunks 40 g
- Onion 100 g
- Cooking oil 10 ml
- Cumin seeds 2 g
- Turmeric powder 1 g
- Salt 4 g

## Method
1. Soak the soy chunks in hot water for 10 minutes, then squeeze out the water.
2. Fry cumin and onion in oil, add soy chunks, turmeric and salt for 2 minutes.
3. Add washed rice and about 300 ml water, close the lid and cook for 2 whistles.
