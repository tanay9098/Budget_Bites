# Aloor dum (spicy potatoes, Bengal style)

Source: Hawkins Pressure Cooker All Indian Cookbook (Hawkins Cookers Ltd.), p. 71. Link: [https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf](https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 8 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Aloor Dum (Spicy Potatoes – Bengal Style)".

**Serves 8. Pressure cooking time 1 minute. Eastern Indian recipe. Equipment: pressure cooker and a deep frying pan.**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (8 servings)
- Onion, 1 medium (100 g)
- Fresh ginger, one 1.5 cm piece (5 g)
- Cloves, 4
- Cinnamon, 3 sticks of 2.5 cm
- Water, 2 cups (480 ml)
- Potatoes, unpeeled, very small (3 to 4 cm diameter), 1.5 kg
- Turmeric, 1 3/4 tsp (8.8 ml)
- Mustard oil, 2 cups (480 ml)
- Bay leaves, 2
- Cumin seeds, 1 tsp (5 ml)
- Onion, 1 medium (100 g), chopped
- Tomato, 1 large (150 g), chopped
- Red chilli powder, 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml)
- Cumin powder, 2 1/2 tsp (12.5 ml)
- Coriander powder, 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml)
- Salt, 1 tbsp (15 ml)
- Sugar, 1 tsp (5 ml)
- Ghee, 2 tbsp (30 ml)

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Grind one onion and the ginger to a paste, and separately grind the cloves and cinnamon to a powder.
2. Put 1 cup (240 ml) of the water in the cooker with the potatoes, close, bring to full pressure on high heat, reduce the heat and cook for 1 minute.
3. Release the pressure by slightly lifting the vent weight, remove and peel the potatoes, sprinkle 3/4 tsp turmeric on them and mix.
4. Fry the potatoes in the hot mustard oil in 3 batches until light brown, remove, and keep 1/4 cup (60 ml) of the strained oil for the gravy.
5. Heat that oil, add bay leaves and cumin, fry the chopped onion until golden brown, add the ground paste and cook about 2 minutes, then add tomato, the spices, salt and sugar, add the potatoes and finish with ghee.
