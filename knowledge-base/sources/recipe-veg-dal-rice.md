# Toor dal tadka with rice

The classic dal-chawal. Dal is pressure-cooked, finished with a cumin tadka, and served over rice cooked separately in the same cooker.

**Serves 2. Prep 10 min, cook 30 min. Equipment: pressure cooker on a gas stove or induction cooktop.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Toor dal 80 g
- Rice 120 g
- Onion 50 g
- Tomato 50 g
- Cooking oil 15 ml
- Cumin seeds 2 g
- Turmeric powder 1 g
- Salt 4 g

## Method
1. Wash dal and cook with turmeric, salt and about 400 ml water for 4 whistles.
2. Cook the washed rice separately with about 300 ml water for 2 whistles.
3. Heat oil, fry cumin, onion and tomato, then stir the tadka into the dal.
