# Khichuri (soft rice with moong dal and vegetables)

Source: Hawkins Pressure Cooker All Indian Cookbook (Hawkins Cookers Ltd.), p. 70. Link: [https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf](https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 8 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Khichuri (Soft Rice with Split Skinned Green Gram and Vegetables)".

**Serves 8. Pressure cooking time 4 minutes. Eastern Indian recipe. Equipment: pressure cooker.**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (8 servings)
- Fresh ginger, one 1.5 cm piece (20 g)
- Garlic, 10 cloves
- Green cardamoms, 6 (seeds taken out and kept, pods discarded)
- Cinnamon, 2 sticks of 2.5 cm
- Cloves, 4
- Water, 8 cups plus 1 tsp (1.9 litres)
- Moong dal, 1 cup (200 g), not washed
- Mustard oil, 1/2 cup (120 ml)
- Bay leaves, 4
- Cumin seeds, 2 tsp (10 ml)
- Onion, 1 large (150 g), chopped
- Basmati rice, 1 cup (200 g), washed and drained
- Cauliflower, 1 small head (300 g), cut into florets of about 2.5 cm
- Potatoes, 2 medium (200 g), cut into 4 cm long, 4 cm wide and 2 cm thick pieces
- Peas, 1 cup (150 g), shelled or frozen
- Turmeric, 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml)
- Coriander powder, 2 tbsp (30 ml)
- Red chilli powder, 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml)
- Sugar, 2 tsp (10 ml)
- Salt, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp (20 ml)
- Ghee, 2 tbsp (30 ml)

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Grind the ginger and garlic to a paste, and separately grind the cardamom seeds, cinnamon and cloves to a powder.
2. Dry-roast the unwashed moong dal on medium heat, stirring, for about 7 minutes until golden brown, then cool it, wash and drain.
3. Heat the oil in the open cooker, add bay leaves and cumin, then fry the chopped onion until golden brown and add the ginger-garlic paste.
4. Add the dal, rice and all remaining ingredients except the water, the ground spices and the ghee, then add the water and stir.
5. Close the cooker, bring to full pressure on high heat, then reduce the heat and cook for 4 minutes.
6. Cool the cooker naturally, open it, stir in the ground spices, and pour hot ghee over the khichuri before serving.
