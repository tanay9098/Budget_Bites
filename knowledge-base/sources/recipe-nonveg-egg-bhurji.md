# Egg bhurji

Spiced scrambled eggs with onion and tomato. Contains eggs, so it is NOT vegetarian in BudgetBites.

**Serves 2. Prep 5 min, cook 10 min. Equipment: stove (gas stove or induction cooktop) and a pan.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Eggs 4 pieces
- Onion 80 g
- Tomato 80 g
- Cooking oil 15 g
- Green chilli 5 g
- Turmeric powder 1 g
- Salt 3 g

## Method
1. Cook onion and chilli in oil until soft, add tomato and turmeric.
2. Beat the eggs with salt, pour in and stir until just set.
