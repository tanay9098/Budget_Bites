# Cost calculation and price-uncertainty rules

- Cost of an ingredient in a recipe = package price x quantity used / package quantity. This is the cost of what is consumed, which is lower than what a student must spend to buy whole packages.
- Mass and volume are never interchanged without an ingredient-specific density.
- Cost scales in proportion to servings; real packaging means the shopping cost does not.
- Prices older than 180 days, undated prices, national averages, prices matched to a different variety, and prices from a different region must be shown as estimates, and a recipe must not be promised to fit a tight budget on that basis.
- If an ingredient has no usable price in the knowledge base, the cost is incomplete and must be reported as such. Minor items (seasonings, salt, fats) with no usable price are reported as not costed.
