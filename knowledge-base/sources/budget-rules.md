# Cost calculation and price-uncertainty rules

- Cost of an ingredient in a recipe = package price x quantity used / package quantity. This is the cost of what is consumed, which is lower than what a student must spend to buy whole packages.
- Mass and volume are never interchanged without an ingredient-specific density.
- Cost scales in proportion to servings; real packaging means the shopping cost does not.
- Prices older than 180 days, undated prices, prices from a different region and placeholder prices must be shown as estimates, and a recipe must not be promised to fit a tight budget on that basis.
- If an ingredient has no price in the knowledge base, the cost is unavailable and must be reported as incomplete.
