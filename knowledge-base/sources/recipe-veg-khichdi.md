# Pressure-cooker moong dal khichdi

A one-pot vegetarian meal for hostel kitchens. Rice and moong dal are cooked together in a pressure cooker, which keeps fuel use and washing-up low.

**Serves 2. Prep 10 min, cook 20 min. Equipment: pressure cooker on a gas stove or induction cooktop.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Rice 100 g
- Moong dal (split, husked) 60 g
- Onion 100 g
- Tomato 100 g
- Cooking oil 10 ml
- Cumin seeds 2 g
- Turmeric powder 2 g
- Salt 4 g
- Water: about 600 ml (not costed)

## Method
1. Rinse rice and moong dal together until the water runs mostly clear.
2. Heat the oil in the open cooker, add cumin, then chopped onion and cook until soft.
3. Add tomato and turmeric and cook for 2 minutes.
4. Add the rinsed rice and dal, salt and water. Close the lid.
5. Cook for 3 whistles on medium heat, then switch off and let the pressure release naturally.
