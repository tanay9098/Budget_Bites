# Kozhi kuttan (Kerala chicken curry)

Source: Hawkins Hevibase instruction manual and recipe book (Issue 2, Sep 2015), p. 49 of the manual. Link: [https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/Hawkins%20Hevibase%20IM.pdf](https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/Hawkins%20Hevibase%20IM.pdf). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 8 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Kozhi Kuttan (Chicken Curry – Kerala Style)".

**Serves 8. Pressure cooking time 7 minutes. South Indian recipe. Contains chicken, so it is not vegetarian. Equipment: pressure cooker.**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (8 servings)
- Chicken pieces, 3 1/4 lb (1.5 kg)
- Turmeric, 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml)
- Salt, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp (20 ml)
- Curd, 1 tbsp (15 ml)
- Coconut, coarsely grated, 2 1/2 cups (200 g)
- Water, 2 1/4 cups (540 ml)
- Coconut oil, 1/4 cup (60 ml)
- Whole dry Kashmiri red chillies, 5
- Whole dry red chillies, 6
- Fresh ginger, 1 x 1 inch (2.5 cm) piece (10 g), chopped
- Garlic, 16 small cloves
- Peppercorns, 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml)
- Aniseed, 1 tsp (5 ml)
- Cinnamon, 1 x 2 inch (5 cm) stick
- Cloves, 10
- Green cardamoms, 3 (seeds taken out and kept, pods discarded)
- Coriander seeds, 1/4 cup + 1 tbsp (25 g)
- Cumin seeds, 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml)
- Onions, 2 medium (250 g), chopped
- Green chillies, 2, slit
- Tomatoes, 2 medium (200 g), puréed

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Rub the chicken with part of the turmeric, part of the salt and the curd and set aside.
2. Extract thick and thin coconut milk from the grated coconut and water.
3. Roast the whole spices, chillies, ginger and garlic in coconut oil for a short time and grind them to a paste with a little water.
4. Fry the onions in the remaining oil in the cooker until golden brown, then add the remaining turmeric, the paste, the green chillies and the tomato purée and cook about 3 minutes.
5. Add the chicken, the remaining salt and the thin coconut milk and stir, close the cooker, bring to full pressure on high heat, reduce the heat and cook 7 minutes.
6. Cool the cooker naturally, open it and stir in the thick coconut milk before serving hot.
