# Pressure-cooker chicken curry

A simple onion-tomato chicken curry. Chicken is the most expensive ingredient and sets the cost of the dish.

**Serves 2. Prep 15 min, cook 25 min. Equipment: pressure cooker on a gas stove or induction cooktop.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Chicken (curry cut) 300 g
- Onion 150 g
- Tomato 150 g
- Cooking oil 20 ml
- Turmeric powder 2 g
- Salt 5 g

## Method
1. Brown the onion in oil in the open cooker, add tomato, turmeric and salt.
2. Add the chicken and about 100 ml water, close the lid.
3. Cook for 3 whistles, then release pressure and reduce the gravy.
