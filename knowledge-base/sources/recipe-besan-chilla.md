# Besan chilla (savoury gram flour pancakes)

Source: Besan Chilla (Savory Gram Flour Pancakes) - Indian Veggie Delight, web page. Link: [https://www.indianveggiedelight.com/besan-chilla/](https://www.indianveggiedelight.com/besan-chilla/). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 4 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Besan Chilla (Savory Gram Flour Pancakes)".

**Serves 4 as published. Prep 10 minutes, cook 15 minutes. Equipment: stove and a tawa or non-stick pan (no pressure cooker).**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (4 servings)
- Besan (gram flour), 1 cup
- Ajwain (carom seeds), 1/2 tsp
- Turmeric, 1/4 tsp
- Red chilli powder, 1/2 tsp (optional)
- Onion, 1/4 cup, finely chopped
- Tomato, 1/4 cup, finely chopped
- Green chilli, 1, finely chopped (optional)
- Ginger, 1 tsp, grated
- Coriander leaves, 2 tbsp
- Salt, to taste
- Water, 1/2 cup, or as needed
- Oil, to cook the chillas

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Mix the besan with the chopped onion, tomato, green chilli, ginger, coriander leaves, ajwain, turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
2. Whisk in water gradually to a smooth, lump-free, pourable batter.
3. Heat an iron tawa or non-stick pan on medium heat and grease it lightly.
4. Pour a ladleful of batter, spread it thin and round, drizzle a little oil round the edge, and cook about 1 to 2 minutes until the base is light golden.
5. Flip and cook the other side until it shows golden spots; serve hot.
