# Besan chilla

Savoury gram-flour pancakes cooked on a tawa or flat pan. No pressure cooker and no rice needed, so it suits breakfast and snacks.

**Serves 2. Prep 10 min, cook 15 min. Equipment: stove (gas stove or induction cooktop) and a flat pan.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Besan (gram flour) 100 g
- Onion 50 g
- Tomato 50 g
- Green chilli 5 g
- Cooking oil 10 g
- Salt 3 g
- Turmeric powder 1 g

## Method
1. Whisk besan with salt, turmeric and about 200 ml water into a pourable batter.
2. Stir in chopped onion, tomato and chilli.
3. Spread a ladle of batter on an oiled hot pan, cook both sides until golden.
