# Tuvar dal (Gujarati style)

Source: Hawkins Pressure Cooker All Indian Cookbook (Hawkins Cookers Ltd.), p. 75. Link: [https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf](https://www.hawkinscookers.com/Cookbooks/AllIndianCookbook.pdf). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 8 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Tuvar Dal (Split Skinned Red Gram – Gujarati Style)".

**Serves 8. Pressure cooking time 3 minutes. Western Indian recipe. Equipment: pressure cooker.**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (8 servings)
- Water, 7 cups (1.7 litres)
- Oily tuvar dal, 1 1/2 cups (300 g)
- Turmeric, 1 tsp (5 ml)
- Salt, 1 tbsp + 2 1/2 tsp (27.5 ml)
- Red chilli powder, 1 1/2 tsp (7.5 ml)
- Coriander powder, 1 1/2 tbsp (22.5 ml)
- Jaggery, shaved and packed, 1/3 cup (65 g)
- Tomato, 1 medium (100 g), chopped
- Green chillies, 3, slit
- Kokum, 7 pieces
- Coriander leaves, 2 tbsp (30 ml), chopped
- Tempering: vegetable oil 2 tbsp (30 ml), mustard seeds 1 tsp (5 ml), cumin seeds 1/2 tsp (2.5 ml), whole dry red chilli 1, curry leaves 1 sprig, asafoetida a large pinch

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Put 4 cups (960 ml) of the water in the cooker with the dal, turmeric and salt and stir.
2. Close the cooker, bring to full pressure on high heat, reduce the heat and cook for 3 minutes, then cool naturally.
3. Open the cooker and mash the dal completely with the back of a ladle.
4. Return to the heat with the remaining water (3 cups) and all other ingredients except the tempering, bring to a boil and simmer for about 5 minutes, stirring now and then.
5. Heat the oil, crackle the mustard seeds, add cumin, red chilli, curry leaves and asafoetida for a few seconds, and pour the tempering over the dal.

## Notes from BudgetBites
- Hawkins advises filling the cooker no more than one-third with dal and water together (see the pressure cooker safety document).
- The recipe is written for a 5 litre cooker. For a 1.5 litre cooker Hawkins says to reduce all ingredients by 80% and increase the pressure-cooking time to 5 minutes; for a 2 litre cooker, increase the time to 4 minutes.
- BudgetBites scales these quantities in proportion to the number of servings you choose; the source gives its pressure-cooking time for the quantity above and states adjustments only for other cooker sizes, so check the source if you cook a very small batch.
