# Onion-tomato-potato pulao

A fast, filling vegetarian rice dish with no dal, for days when pulses are not in the kitchen.

**Serves 2. Prep 10 min, cook 20 min. Equipment: pressure cooker on a gas stove or induction cooktop.**

## Ingredients (for 2 servings)
- Rice 150 g
- Onion 100 g
- Tomato 100 g
- Potato 100 g
- Cooking oil 15 g
- Cumin seeds 2 g
- Green chilli 5 g
- Salt 4 g

## Method
1. Heat oil, add cumin and sliced onion, cook until golden.
2. Add tomato, diced potato and chilli and stir for 2 minutes.
3. Add washed rice, salt and about 300 ml water; close the lid.
4. Cook for 2 whistles, then let the pressure release naturally.

This dish has little protein compared with dal-based meals, because it contains no pulses.
