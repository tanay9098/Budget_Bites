# Pressure cooker technique and safety

General practice for Indian home pressure cookers. Always follow your own cooker's manual, which overrides this note.

## Safety
- Do not fill the cooker more than two-thirds full; fill foods that foam or expand, such as dal and rice, no more than half full.
- Check that the vent pipe and gasket are clean and seated before every use.
- Let pressure release naturally or follow the manual before opening; never force the lid.

## Cooking guidance
- Rice and moong dal together: about 3 whistles on medium heat, then natural release.
- Plain rice: about 2 whistles.
- Toor dal: about 4 whistles.
- Using the cooker saves fuel compared with open-pot boiling of dal.
