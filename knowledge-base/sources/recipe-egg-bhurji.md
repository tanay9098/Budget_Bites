# Egg bhurji (Indian scrambled eggs)

Source: Egg Bhurji Recipe (Indian Anda Bhurji) - Swasthi's Recipes, web page. Link: [https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/egg-bhurji-andhra-egg-porutu/](https://www.indianhealthyrecipes.com/egg-bhurji-andhra-egg-porutu/). The ingredient quantities below are transcribed from the source for 2 servings; the method is paraphrased by BudgetBites. Recipe title in the source: "Egg Bhurji Recipe (Indian Anda Bhurji)".

**Serves 2 as published. Prep 5 minutes, cook 15 minutes. Contains eggs, so it is not vegetarian. Equipment: stove and a pan.**

## Ingredients as stated in the source (2 servings)
- Eggs, 4 (or 2 to 3 jumbo eggs)
- Salt, 1/4 teaspoon
- Oil, 2 tablespoons
- Cumin seeds, 1/2 teaspoon
- Onion, 1/2 to 3/4 cup, finely chopped
- Green chilli, 1, slit or chopped
- Ginger garlic paste, 3/4 to 1 teaspoon
- Garam masala, 3/4 to 1 teaspoon
- Red chilli powder, 1/2 to 3/4 teaspoon
- Turmeric, 1/8 teaspoon
- Coriander leaves, 2 tablespoons
- Tomato, 1 small, finely chopped (optional)
- Bell pepper (capsicum), 1/4 cup (optional)

## Method (paraphrased)
1. Beat the eggs with the salt and turmeric until frothy and set aside.
2. Heat the oil, add cumin seeds and, when they splutter, fry the onion and green chilli until the onion is golden, then the ginger garlic paste for a minute.
3. Add the tomato (and bell pepper, if using) and cook until soft.
4. Pour in the eggs and scramble lightly, cooking until the eggs are soft and then fully cooked.
5. Stir in the garam masala and red chilli powder for a minute or two, adjust the salt, and garnish with coriander leaves.
